# q007 Haradirovo selatko, Kniha inskripce, Adamantinove kladivko

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q007.cs`, `Q007Dialogs.cs`, `Q007Pages.cs`, `q007.def`, from Erebor's
`questy/q007_HaradirovoSelatko.scp` (#1266, step 14 of #1237). Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Three journal entries.

`Q007Pages.cs` holds Erebor's old-style dialogs page for page, its texts byte for byte, generated from the archive once (as the Grandmaster
dialogs were) and meant to be read like normal code. Which one a player sees and what its buttons do is in `Q007Dialogs.cs`, as
Erebor's `f_q007_*_DClick` functions had it. The innkeeper's dialog decides page by page, so it's written by hand there.

## a, Haradirovo selatko (Haradir's piglet)

Anyone, once. Stage `Q007.GetStage` (Erebor's `qtag(q007_haradirovo_selatko)`).

| Stage | How it's reached | Experience |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Haradir, a hunter in the north, asks for the smoked piglet the innkeeper owes him | |
| 2 | The innkeeper denies everything (any of the ways to give up arguing) | |
| 3 | Haradir names the witch Rachel | |
| 4 | Rachel wants 1000 bat wings (`i_reag_Batwing`) | |
| 5 | The bat wings handed over: she "talks" to the innkeeper | |
| 6 | The innkeeper confesses he fears the Evil Dryad of his island, and wants her bow as proof | |
| 7 | Her bow handed in: the piglet | 500 |
| 8 | The piglet to Haradir, who tells of the old smith and the inscripter (b and c) | 500 |

The Evil Dryad (`c_Zla_Dryada`, 4000-5000 hits, Archery 150) carries her bow as a quest item: the first player to open her corpse has it.

**The innkeeper** also sells drinks, one every 5 minutes whatever it was, through `GoldUtils.Pay`: wine 150, liquor 200, ale 100, cider 200.
Erebor took 200 for the cider while its menu said 250; here it's Erebor's 200 (#1307, the user's call). He carries Eriol's line for q006 too. His q017 lines (Terlak's protection
potion) come with q017.

**Rodrigo**, a pirate near Umbar, sells rumours: 10,000 gold, or 8,000 after haggling. His news says where the witch spends the season
and that the stolen stones are on the pirates' island in the north. For 50,000 more he "adopts a dolphin" once, for 200 experience.

## b, Kniha inskripce (the Inscription book) and the magic pen

After a, a player with Inscription above 79.9 (`Q007.GetInscripter`, Erebor's `q007_stary_inscripter`):

1. The Old Inscripter asks for three stones: a moon stone, a meteorite and a blood stone.
2. The stones for the book: 2000 experience.
3. Optional: the drunk apprentice Divous trades his magic pen for the book. There's no way back.

**The book** is what lets a player inscribe 8th-circle scrolls (`Q007.MayInscribe`, in Inscription's `CanScribe` and `CanStartMaking`), as
Erebor's craft menu wanted `i_book_inscription` for that circle. Without it the 8th circle isn't offered. GMs don't need it.
The archive's recipes for those eight scrolls asked for the book as an item in the pack; the converter leaves it out of their
SKILLMAKE now (`ConvertedRecipes`, a comment line marks each), as the book is a tag.

**The pen** turns a blank rune (`i_rune_tinker`, Tinkering) into a working recall rune (`i_rune_marker`) when the player double-clicks the
rune with Inscription above 84.9. So the choice is Erebor's: the 8th circle, or making recall runes.

## c, Adamantinove kladivko (the adamantine hammer)

After a, a smith with Blacksmithing above 99.9 (`Q007.GetSmith`, Erebor's `q007_prastary_kovar`):

1. The Ancient Smith asks for the same three stones.
2. The stones for the hammer: 2000 experience.

**The hammer** (`i_adamantinum_kladivko`) is a smith's hammer, an item: it works as the smithing tool and gives +10.0 Blacksmithing (a skill
modifier) while held by a smith at 100, which reaches the 110.0 that mithril armour needs. Taking it off takes the bonus back. It isn't bound to
an owner, as on Erebor.

## The stones

Erebor had no source for them but a GM's loot template (`TM_poklad_piratu`, "nepouzivat!"). Here **the pirates' chest** (`i_q007_poklad_piratu`)
on the pirates' island (`a_Ostrov_Piratu`), where Rodrigo's rumour puts them, gives all three (as tags) to a player sent for them by the
inscripter or the smith who doesn't have them yet, once no hostile creature stands within 10 tiles (two pirates spawn beside it). A player
doing both b and c comes back for a second set.

## Quest items

The bow, the piglet, the three stones, the book and the pen are player tags (`QuestItems`), the quest-item rule. Erebor's blood stone also
joined `i_krvavy_mec` into a blood sword (uniques.scp); as a tag it doesn't. Two archive recipes still ask for a quest item as an item and so can't be
met, neither offered by any craft menu: `i_rune_marker`'s SKILLMAKE wants `i_magicke_pero` (the pen makes recall runes its own way, above),
and `i_forge`'s RESOURCES want `i_meteorit`.

## Differences from Erebor

- The quest items are tags; the stones have a source (the chest).
- The journal's pen text named Rodrigo, it was Divous; and Erebor's journal showed the book's text again after the trade, so its pen text
  never showed. Both are fixed.
- Haradir's "any news" button set a `q007_haradir_Nemethen` tag a thread never continued; only his line is kept.
- The cider's price, see above.
- The Dryad doesn't hold her bow (it's a tag on her, for the corpse), so she fights with what she has.
