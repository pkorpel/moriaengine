# q026 Otrava z hrobek (The tomb poison)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q026.cs` (+ two empty typedefs in `q026.scp`), from Erebor's `questy/q026_hcnPoison.scp` and
`_items.scp`, #1272, step 17 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Otrava z hrobek", Erebor's texts.
Once per character; the reward is the cure: the curse ends and never comes back.

## The curse

The HCN tombs' wraiths (`c_hcn_wraith_level_1..4`, ability `a_tevent_e_q026_hcnpoisoning_npc`) curse a hero with their first landed
blow (`Q026.WraithBlow`, in `DamageManager.ProcessSwing` before the damage). After that:

- 7 of their blows in 10 on a cursed hero are up to 5/3 stronger ("Bolest z otravy prochazi celym tvym telem.");
- every 400-800 s (in the world only) the curse takes a food point, or 25 damage at 4 food or less (`Q026CurseTimer`);
- a cure potion (lesser, normal, greater) doesn't help: drinking one moves the quest on (stage 2).

A cured hero (stage 15) is immune: no curse, no stronger blows.

## Differences from Erebor

- Eriol is the healer, and he answers at any q006 stage. Erebor's other healers (q010's Gregoriel, q013's experimenter) aren't ported,
  and Eriol asked only once q006 had started.
- Andor and Roxana have only their q026 lines and their plain pages; their own quests (q016, q020, q022) aren't ported.
- The key and the book are stages, as Erebor's; the cure is drunk at Roxana's (Erebor's stage 14, a cure in the pack, is skipped:
  quest items are tags, #1090's rule).
- The curse is a timer on the hero, not a memory item, and doesn't set the poisoned flag (it isn't a poison here).
- The hidden room's GM machinery (floors that show and hide as players step in) isn't ported: the wall only slides aside. The temple's
  second door half and the GM setup dialogs aren't either.
- The tile in Dol Guldur is invisible, as Erebor's (attr 090); the box is a container players could drop things into but nobody can
  open (as Erebor's); the fixtures answer a double click from afar (Telekinesis) as Erebor's did, only the book pile wants the hero close.
- Erebor's wraiths skip a hero with an energy shield up (`tag.energyofshield`), here too since #1401.
- Erebor's debug lines ("found: N of X", "consuming...") are gone; stage 5 (Unas on the Bystra) was never set, its text stays.
