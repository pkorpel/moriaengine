# q041 Paserak (the smuggler)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q041.cs` (hand-ported from Erebor's `questy/prestupak.scp`, `prestupak_chars.scp` and the gate and
portal of `zmodrani.scp`), #1278, step 20 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Paserak", Erebor's
texts. Repeatable, dearer each time.

## The story

The smuggler (`c_q041_prestupak_paserak`) takes a hero from one realm to the other. He talks only to Gondor and Mordor heroes of level
15 and up; outcasts and the weak get "Prijd, az budes silnejsi!", those of no realm "S tebou se ani ja bavit nebudu!".

1. "Muzu pomoci?": he wants 10 000 gold before talking (paid through `GoldUtils.Pay`).
2. "Prechod?": the hero says they really want to change.
3. The proof:
   - a **Gondor** hero kills the innocent elf (`c_q041_prestupak_elf`) by Rivendell; the elf's death moves the killer on (a pet's kill
     counts for its master);
   - a **Mordor** hero brings farmer Borek (`c_mq002_borek`) by Nurn 40 cooked fish steaks (`i_fish_cut_cooked`) and 5 refresh potions
     (`i_potion_refresh`), handed over in Borek's dialog. Borek sends Gondor heroes away ("Zmiz, gondorsky pse!").
4. The price: 10 iron ingots, a magic bag (`i_mi_bag`) for each realm change so far plus one (at most 10), a roast pig and a bank
   transfer of level x 10 000 + 1 000 000 gold (from the bank only, as Erebor's `pay_from_bank`, through `GoldUtils.Pay`).
5. His portal (`i_port_prechod_2`) takes a hero of level 15 at that point (and a GM) to beside the realm gate, not onto it: arriving
   on a step trigger fires it, and the change should be the hero's own step. Anyone else lands on the island's shore (1142,3897,
   "Svatyne te neprijala.").
6. The realm gate (`i_level_gate`) changes the realm and sends the hero home:
   - Mordor to Gondor: murders and karma to 0, home Minas Tirith;
   - Gondor to Mordor: karma -3000, home Barad-dur.
   It counts the change (Erebor's `tag.presel`, which [q30](q30.md) reads: a hero who changed realm starts the other envoy's ladder
   with half experience). Outcasts are refused for good. After a change the gate leaves the hero alone for a minute.

## Setting it up

`.SetupQuestPorts`: the smuggler, his portal and the realm gate on Ostrov nestalosti (the island Erebor's portal sends the refused to),
the elf a little south of Rivendell (a spawn, so he comes back), Borek on a field west of Nurn. Erebor's GMs placed them all; the archive
keeps no spot. Unreviewed.

## Differences from Erebor

- The 10 000 goes through `GoldUtils.Pay` from the pack, then the bank (Erebor took it from the pack only).
- Mordor's 30 000 kills marked Mordor's side on Erebor; here the realm alone decides, so the gate doesn't set murders.
- Borek's dialog is only his greeting, the pages a hero who hasn't begun mq002 sees, and q041's bargain: mq002 itself isn't ported.
- The gate's fire columns are one fire effect; the GM's account message is a log line.
- As on Erebor, the change touches only realm, karma, murders and home: a town office, citizenship, a ring, a realm uniform or a keep
  captaincy of the old realm stays with the hero (a question for the user).
