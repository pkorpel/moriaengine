# q009 Mistr krejci (The master tailor)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q009.cs`, pages in `Q009Pages.cs` (generated from Erebor's `questy/q009_mistr_krejci.scp`, text for
text), #1270, step 16 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Mistr krejci" (Erebor had none, the texts are
new). Once per character.

## The story

Talathwen (`c_q009_krejci`), a tailor near bankruptcy, sells her family's ring to someone who flatters her dress rather than her shop.

1. Agreeing (`krejci_1:1`): she wants a cashier's cheque (`i_cashiers_check`) of **exactly 5,000,000** gold. A cheque for any other sum
   doesn't count. Erebor looked only at the first cheque in the pack, and so does this port.
2. Handing it over (`krejci_3:1`): the cheque is taken and the ring is ready "tomorrow": Erebor's 86400 of `serv.time`, which counts
   tenths, so **2.4 hours** (8640 s on the server's clock, which runs only while the server is up).
3. Asking for it before then **puts it off by 18 minutes** (Erebor's 10800 tenths, added when her dialog opens). Leaving politely
   (`krejci_4:1`) brings it **6 minutes nearer**.
4. Ready (`krejci_5:1`): the ring (`i_q009_ring_tailor`), engraved with the hero's name. Below Tailoring 100 she warns that the money may
   have been wasted, as on Erebor.
5. After that: her farewell dialog.

Every one of her dialogs has a "Obchodovat?" button that opens the shop (`D_VendorShop`), as Erebor's `buy`.

## The ring

- Only its owner can wear it ("Tento prsten ti nepadne. Je na nem vyryto jmeno ..."), and only at Tailoring 100. Refused in
  `Item.On_DenyEquip` (`Q009.RefusesEquip`), by the wearer's rules, also when someone else drops it on their paperdoll.
- Worn: **+10.0 Tailoring** (a skill modifier, `Character.On_ItemEquip`); taken off: back.
- It's a real item, not a tag: it's worn.
