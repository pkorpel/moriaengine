# q30 Zmocnenec Gondoru / Mordoru (The realm's envoy)

54,650 experience in all. The last hunt gives the **warrior's cloak** ("Plast bojovnika", `i_plast_bojovnika`): +10 hit point regeneration,
+20% against dragons (magic item values 70 and 102), the realm's colour (Gondor 0x098F, Mordor 0x0993), made by its owner (no magic-item
exchange takes it), and only its owner can wear it ("Nemuzes nosit tenhle plast").

**A lost cloak:** a hero who is done asks again ("Ztratil jsem svuji odmenu"): the last hunt for 8, then 12 dragon hearts, no experience,
another cloak.

## Realms

Each envoy speaks to his own realm only ("Vyhni se memu meci, prisluhovaci ...!"). A hero who changed realm (Erebor's `tag.presel`, here
`Q30.SwitchedRealmTK`, which q041's realm change will set) leaves the other ladder on the first visit, remembering how far they got; they
earn half experience until they're past that point. A thief who began the other ladder isn't taken at all ("Jdi pryc").

## Differences from Erebor

- Mordor's journal page had its last two texts swapped; it follows Gondor's pattern.
- The repeat's bigger bag is only the last hunt's: Erebor set it on whatever bag a hero with a cloak took, a realm changer's first hunt too.
- A hunt taken again replaces the old bag. The bags are newbie and fixed in the pack, as the other quest bags.
- The cloak's durability reset on equip isn't ported (cloaks don't wear here).
- As on Erebor, a hero who loses a hunt's bag is stuck on it; a GM resets the stage tag (`_gq030Zmocnenec_` / `_mq030Zmocnenec_`).
