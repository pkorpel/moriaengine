# q017 Alchymista Terlak (Alchemist Terlak)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q017.cs`, and the innkeeper's q017 pages in `Q007Dialogs.cs`, from Erebor's
`questy/q017_alchymista_terlak.scp` and q007's file (#1268, step 15 of #1237). Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry
"Alchymista Terlak". Repeatable: every run raises the cost.

## The potion

After q007's piglet (stage above 6), the innkeeper tells of his old friend, the alchemist Terlak, and the protection potion they brewed against
Terlak's guardian spell (`Q017.GetProtection`: 1 = asked for, 2 = drunk and working).

- **The first potion is free** (+200 experience), while the player has neither a potion nor a finished run.
- **Every later one**: his bag (`i_q017_kolektor_srnky`, newbie and fixed in the pack) filled with 200 deer eyes, one from each deer
  ("Body of Srnec"), and 10,000 gold through `GoldUtils.Pay`. +200 experience. A lost bag can be asked for again.
- A run used up the potion.

## The run

**The entry gate** (`i_scout_vstupni_gate`) wants:

1. Level 35. A lower player is pushed back a step.
2. Nobody else through it in the last 4 hours.
3. Red and blue gate crystals: one of each the first time, then as many of each as the run count (`Q017.GetRuns`: 2, 4, 6...).
4. The potion. Without it the gate hurts (30, later 24) and refuses.

It takes the crystals and the potion, and sends the hero to Gargamel's dungeon (`a_Gargamelova_kobka`, 5719,111 on plane 10). Then it jumps to
another of its four dungeons: Etheria level 2, Snagum level 1, Arachnon level 1 or Mount Dorlas level 2.

**Inside:** Gargamel the skull collector (`c_crunch_zoldak`) guards it, and a 4-hour limit runs (`Q017RunTimer`). It counts only time in the
game, as Erebor's timer item didn't tick for someone logged out. It's told every 10 minutes, then every half minute in the last two. Running
out of time, or dying, ends the run at 1350,3027. A dead hero's corpse stays inside, reachable only by another run, as on Erebor.

**The exit gate** (`i_scout_vystupni_gate`) at the far end gives the hero's profession its item, raises the run count by 2 and sends them back
where they came in.

## The crystals

Erebor had no source for them: GMs or events handed them out. Here a monster killed in one of the gate's four dungeons, by a player on the
quest (not during a run), carries one, red or blue at even odds. The chance is on `.settings` -> "Questy" -> `terlakCrystalChance`, default 3%.
They're counted tags, the quest-item rule.

## The collector

The collector (`c_q017_sberatel`) greets and asks for Terlak's artifacts. Erebor's bought only the **old** versions of them: an item named as
the current reward got "Tyto novy nesbiram, jenom stary!". So with only the current items in the world he never buys, here as there. Erebor's
purchase (a 50,000 cheque, the run count down by 2) isn't ported.

| The collector | by Minas Tirith's spawn point (no spot on Erebor) |

## Differences from Erebor

- The gate stamps its 4 hours on every entry. Erebor's stamped only later runs, so first-timers could follow each other in.
- A reward is refused at equip time. Erebor put it on and took it off again (or bounced the other reward worn).
- The crystals have a source (above); they and the potion are tags.
- The run's time is told less often (above).
- The collector, see above.
- Gargamel isn't reset at each entry (Erebor's `link.timer=1`); his spawn brings him back a minute or two after a death.

## Test

`Tests/Protocol/Q017Tests.cs` (+ `scripts/Tests/Q017Tests.cs`): a client greets the innkeeper and drinks the free potion. The server checks:

- later potions want the deer eyes and gold, and the free one isn't free again;
- every rule of the entry gate, the lockout, and later runs wanting more crystals;
- the exit's warrior shield with its values, and that it's kept from the exchange;
- the rewards' wearing rules, through the item's own @DenyEquip, also dropped on by someone else;
- a lost deer-eye bag asked for again, and only one at a time;
- the crystals dropping only in the four dungeons and only for a player on the quest;
- a run ending by time-out and by death, each on its timer.
