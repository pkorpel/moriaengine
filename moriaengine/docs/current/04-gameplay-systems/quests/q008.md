# q008 Black Mustang (Cerny mustang)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q008.cs`, pages in `Q008Pages.cs` (generated from Erebor's `questy/q008_black.scp`, text for text),
#1274, step 18 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Cerny mustang" (Erebor had none, the texts are new).
Repeatable: each new black mustang is another chance.

## The story

The old horse breeder Ancam (`c_q008_old_man`) won't sell his black stallion, but he lets a ranger try to tame one if they bring what's
needed to raise another. Only **rangers with Taming and Animal Lore 95.0** get past his small talk; a greedy answer ("Toho kone chci!",
or asking for another mustang the wrong way) bans the hero for good ("Nejsi zde vitan!").

1. Take his crate (`i_q008_benda_regy`, newbie and fixed in the pack): a double click asks for a reagent within 3 tiles in sight. Any
   reagent counts; **2500** are counted, and the deposit after that fills the crate (Erebor's literal rule).
2. Kill the four snow-island monsters (`c_q008_elem_snow`, `c_q008_elem_ice`, `c_q008_eye_snow`, `c_q008_eye_ice`) while holding the
   crate (stage 1, as Erebor's): each death tags its killer, or a pet's master (`NPC.On_Death`, whatever the monster is worth in
   experience).
3. Bring both back. With the mustang in the corral, the gate is the hero's (stage 2). Without one, Ancam keeps the crate and waits; once a
   mustang has come, he sends the hero (+400 experience).
4. The corral: the outside pad unlocks the gate for a minute, for a hero at stage 2 only; the inside pad locks it behind them ("Ted jiz
   neni cesty zpet!", +100 experience, stage 3). Only a hero locked in may tame the corral's mustang (`C_A_MUSTANG_BLACK`, a magic mount,
   #925; anyone else hears "Tento mustang patri Ancamovi."). With it tamed (or ridden), or gone, the gate lets them out and the outside pad
   ends the quest (stage 4) and closes it. The gate's lock has its own id, so no key opens it, the open-door macro included.
5. A new mustang in the corral: Ancam offers another crate.

"The mustang is there" means an untamed black mustang within 30 tiles (Erebor's link to the mustang spawn's last creature).

## Differences from Erebor

- The map has no fence there: the corral is only the gate, its pads and the rule that only a hero locked in may tame the mustang. The
  mustang's spawn brings a new one a minute or two after the last is tamed.
- The gate opens only at stage 2 while freshly unlocked, or to let a stage-3 hero out; Erebor's opened for anyone while unlocked, until
  its timer re-locked it.
- The inside pad locks the gate behind a hero at stage 2 only. Erebor's ran for anyone not at stage 3, so someone without the quest
  slipping in got stage 3 and 100 experience.
- Each button works only from the dialog that shows it.
- The kill tags go to the killer through the NPC's death, as Erebor's `act`.
- The mustang check is by distance, not a spawn link a GM had to set (`tag mustspawnid`); the second gate (`i_q008_vrata_2`) and
  `i_dlazka_rmpeacemade` aren't placed.
- A journal entry.
