# q099 Vyvrhel (the outcast)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q099.cs` (hand-ported from Erebor's `questy/q099_vyvrhel.scp` and `q099_vyvrhel_items.scp`), #1278,
step 20 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Vyvrhel", Erebor's texts.

## The story

Podivin (`c_q099_vyvrhel`) frees an outcast (Gondor's or Mordor's) of their exile. Anyone else hears "Di pryc! Nic nemam a nic
nechci!". His four tasks, each a collector bag (#1247), newbie and fixed in the pack:

1. 100 grizzly tails (`i_q099_vyvrhel_1`),
2. 80 earth elemental ears (`i_q099_vyvrhel_2`),
3. 50 eye essences (`i_q099_vyvrhel_3`),
4. 5 poison phoenix feathers (`i_q099_vyvrhel_4`).

Then Mining 40 ("Bez kopat!"), and a cashier's cheque of exactly 250 000 ("To je malo!" for any other). The cheque and **10 levels** (#1307, the user's call; Erebor took 15) buy
the realm back (Erebor's function `vyvrhel`): a Gondor outcast becomes Gondor's with no murders, fame or karma, a Mordor outcast
Mordor's with no fame and -3000 karma. A hero exiled again can ask again.

## Setting it up

`.SetupQuestPorts`: Podivin beside the smuggler of [q041](q041.md) on Ostrov nestalosti. Erebor's GMs placed him; the archive keeps no
spot. Unreviewed.

## Differences from Erebor

- Erebor also wanted `tag.vykoupeni`, which nothing in the archive sets: here every outcast may ask.
- `srazitlevely`, which Erebor called, isn't in the archive: here 10 levels down (not below 1) and the experience toward the next level
  gone. Since #1307 (the user's call) the points those levels gave are taken back, spent or not, so levels earned again pay fairly:
  - **skill points** (5 a level) from what's unspent, then from the steps bought with points, 5.0 at a time, the highest first,
    each step refunding what it cost; a step that costs more than is still owed gives the change back. Only steps bought with
    points count: never a master's step (from 25.0, 50.0 or 75.0, taught for gold) nor what use taught (a value that isn't a whole
    5.0). What can't be taken back is forgiven;
  - **attribute points** from the stats, a point at a time from the highest, never under the profession's minimum, until the lower
    level's budget balances (hits and stamina come down to the new maximums);
  - **class abilities** count their points from the level, so if they're over, they reset to be spent again.
  - Podivin waits while Bless, Curse or the like, or `.honor` / `.fear`, change the stats: the stats taken back are the real ones.
- Nothing renames an outcast here, so the name isn't put back.
- Erebor's own gump becomes a quest dialog: the greeting on its first page, the task behind "Ukol".
- The first bag, not yet full, gets "Hm, jeste toho nemas dost!" as the others did (Erebor said nothing).
- Erebor removed the quest at the end and its journal called it done already when ready (stage 9); here it is done after the
  redemption and stays so (a new text, "Podivin te zbavil vyhnanstvi.") until a new exile.
- The level loss takes only unspent points: points spent before stay spent, and the levels earned back give their points again (a
  question for the user).
