# q018 Zlaty losos (The golden salmon)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q018.cs`, from Erebor's `questy/q018_zlaty_losos.scp`, dialogs hand-ported (their pages change by
stage), #1270, step 16 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Zlaty losos", Erebor's texts. Once per character.

## The story

`Q018.GetStage`, Erebor's `qtag q018`:

1. The fisherman (`c_q018_rybar`) can't catch the river's giant salmon; a fly of golden thread would do. Taking the task gives a small fish
   and sends the hero to Mirrana.
2. Mirrana (`c_q018_mirrana`) knows where the magic spinning wheel is, but a lizard's bite festers: she sends the hero to the salve-maker.
3. Elnassir (`c_q018_elnassir`) makes the salve from **20 spider silk, 80 nightshade, 150 bones and 10,000 gold** (`GoldUtils.Pay`). The
   salve (`i_q018_mirranin_odvar`) is a quest item, a tag.
4. The salve to Mirrana (+500 experience): she tells of a golden rune in Osgiliath's eastern ruins. The rune (`i_q018_portujici_runa`)
   takes a hero at this stage to the Pits of Death by the wheel (5390,35); anyone else can't use it.
5. Touching the wheel (`i_q018_zlate_kolo`): the golden thread (a tag), +1500 experience, **+3.0 Tailoring** on the real value, and back
   to Osgiliath (6008,3860).
6. The thread to the fisherman: +1000 experience and 20,000 gold.

## Differences from Erebor

- The salve and the thread are tags (the quest-item rule).
- The wheel works once, at stage 4. Erebor's had no check: anyone who reached it could spin again and again for +3.0 Tailoring each time.
- The fisherman's fish and the start only at stage 0; Mirrana takes the salve only at stage 3. Erebor's buttons didn't check.
