## Differences from Erebor

- The parchment and the potion of purification are player tags, not items (the quest-item rule). The potion can't be brewed (the
  user's call, 2026-11-07: it's a pure quest item, only from the quest's NPC). On Erebor the alchemist went off to brew
  `i_potion_ocisteni` (Alchemy 100.0), but Erebor's alchemy menu never listed it, so that ending couldn't be reached there.
- The dog is any figurine of a shrunk `c_dog`; Erebor counted its `i_pet_dog` item, which its shrinking made.
- Erebor's "take the ingredients back" answer at stage 22 led to a page it never wrote, so it could never be pressed; here it works
  (as the potion is mixed at Eriol's, it gives the potion rather than the ingredients).
- Qworet's and Unas's own quest (q023, the magic dagger) isn't ported: they have only their plain pages and q006's lines. The
  innkeeper has only his greeting and Eriol's line until q007 (step 14). Eriol's lines for q026 (step 18) and Erebor's q000
  promotion hint aren't here.
- The robe's bonus is a skill modifier and the robe remembers whom it gave it to, so a save between putting it on and taking it off
  is fine. Erebor raised the skill itself and kept a player tag.
