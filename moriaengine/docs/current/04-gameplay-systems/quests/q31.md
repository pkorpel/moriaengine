# q31 Kuchar Mrkvicka (Carrot the cook)

Code: `scripts/Basic/Quests/Ports/Q31.cs`, pages in `Q31Pages.cs` (generated from Erebor's `questy/q31_kuchar.scp`, text for text), #1274,
step 18 of #1237. Setup: `scripts/Setup/QuestPortsSetup.cs`. Journal entry "Kuchar Mrkvicka", Erebor's texts. Once per character.

## The story

The cook (Gondor's `c_q31_kuchar`, Mordor's `c_q31_kuchara`) needs roast birds for a rich client.

- "Rad to udelam": his bag (`i_q31_kuchar_kolektor`, a collector, #1247), newbie and fixed in the pack. It takes the bodies of birds
  ("Body of Ptacek").
- Full (50): **a llama figurine** (a double click brings the llama out as the hero's own) and 50 experience.
- Lost for good ("S tebou se uz nemam o cem bavit"): turning him down, inventing Ariadne's liqueur recipe, or giving up (he keeps the
  bag).
- "!!!VYMENIT NEFUNKCNI PYTLIK ZA NOVY!!!": a fresh, empty bag.

Each cook serves his own side and those of no realm, and shrieks at the other ("AAAAAA, Mordorani !!!").

## Setting it up

`.SetupQuestPorts`: the cooks had no spot on Erebor; Gondor's stands in Pelargir, Mordor's in Harad. Unreviewed.

## Differences from Erebor

- Erebor picked the cook's function by the speaker's realm, not by the cook, so either cook served Gondor; here each serves its own side.
- The figurine is the shrunk-pet figurine holding a llama: Erebor's `i_pet_llama` (`t_figurine`) does nothing here.
- The bag's replacement also gives one to a hero who lost theirs (Erebor's swapped only a bag still held).
